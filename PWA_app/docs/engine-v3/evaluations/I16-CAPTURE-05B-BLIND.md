## Grading rule

For each ad, grade relevance to the target hunt only:

- **3 — Exact/strong match:** offers or seeks whole vehicles, or is clearly a vehicle buy/sell venue matching the broad hunt.
- **2 — Related but incomplete/partial:** genuinely related to whole-vehicle buying/selling, but materially less direct or only partly satisfies the target.
- **1 — Weak/adjacent:** vehicle-related, but the main offer/request is a component, part, or service rather than whole-vehicle buying/selling.
- **0 — Irrelevant:** does not meaningfully match the target.
- **Unknown:** insufficient evidence to decide.

Do not lower relevance because of vehicle condition, defects, price, seller quality, or ad quality. Judge what the ad is primarily offering/seeking. Please return one grade per blind ID, with a brief reason only where useful.
